# Mysticism: Magic Armory

A focused NeoForge 1.21.1 equipment and construction add-on for Mysticism.

## Acryl

Acryl is crafted from four Nether Quartz, four Popped Chorus Fruit, and one Echo Shard, producing two Acryl. The Warden continues to drop renewable Echo Shards.

Acryl equipment has diamond-level base statistics, slightly reduced durability, high enchantability, translucent dyeable material sections, and treats leveled enchantments as one level higher during gameplay.

## Magic focuses

Staffs are focused beam weapons. Piercing increases the number of targets a beam can strike, and its colored trail stops at the final target actually hit. Tomes are aimed area-of-effect weapons with one shared four-block radius at every tier. Tomes retain their flat icon in inventories and animate their covers and pages only while held. Their progression uses ordinary Minecraft materials rather than introducing additional material families:

- Staffs: Flint, Amethyst, Diamond, Emerald, Netherite, Acryl
- Tomes: Leather, Amethyst, Diamond, Emerald, Netherite, Acryl

There is no Lapis tier, wand family, or runic ingredient tier.

### Focus enchantments

Arcane Reach applies to staffs and tomes. Staffs use Arcane Impact alongside compatible vanilla weapon enchantments; Arcane Impact conflicts with Channeling. Tomes support Flame, Wind Burst, Breach, and Invocation. Invocation consumes a supported offhand catalyst, drains all current mana, and places every tome on a sixty-second cooldown. Supported catalysts include potions, ender pearls, TNT, arrows, fireworks, and milk buckets.

## Construction

Opaque and partially translucent Acryl families exist in the natural color and all sixteen dye colors. Opaque Acryl has blocks, bricks, chiseled blocks, pillars, smooth blocks, stairs, slabs, and walls. Regular Acryl uses a smooth bordered shell surface; Smooth Acryl removes that border. Acryl Glass has regular, brick, and chiseled blocks; matching panes for each; stairs; and slabs.

Matching-colored Acryl Glass full blocks and panes suppress internal faces. Every Acryl construction block is Wither-immune, Ender-Dragon-immune, diamond-tool gated, and highly blast resistant. Related color recipes share recipe-book groups by shape.


## Beacon and glass behavior

All transparent Acryl forms tint beacon beams in their Acryl color, while every full opaque Acryl block form is accepted as a beacon-base block. Acryl Glass drops itself without Silk Touch. Smooth Acryl Glass and its pane variant provide borderless window options. Acryl Pillars use a vertically seamless spiral texture.
